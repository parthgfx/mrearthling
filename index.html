The lore

Who is Mr. Earthling?

Nobody knows exactly where he's from. We do know that Mr. Earthling, or Ear for short (say it like "err"), has a lot of ears, a big appetite, and zero interest in fitting in.

He came for the planet and stayed for the cabbage. Now he ferments it the slow way, with real veggies and live cultures, and listens very closely to your gut.

Here's the part Ear is proudest of. Every jar is made by a homemaker who, later in life, is starting her very first business. Each one you buy helps her build something of her own, one step closer to full independence. Ear calls that the least normal, most impressive thing on this planet.

Questions from earthlings

How do I order?
On WhatsApp. Tap any "Get the jar" button, send the message that pops up, and we'll take it from there. We don't take orders on the website.
What is Mr. Earthling kimchi?
A naturally fermented kimchi made the Indian way. Each 250 g jar is packed with live probiotic cultures, napa cabbage, carrots, radish, gochujang and more, with no artificial preservatives.
How much does it cost?
₹330 per 250 g jar at our launch price (usually ₹500). Delivery is charged separately at actual cost, through Porter or Swiggy Genie.
Where do you deliver, and how fast?
We deliver in Bengaluru (Bangalore). Your order is dispatched the day after you place it on WhatsApp.
What's actually in the jar?
Napa cabbage, carrots, radish, gochujang, rice paste, soy sauce, garlic, ginger, salt and pear or apple. That's the whole list. No artificial preservatives.
Is Mr. Earthling kimchi vegan?
Yes. It is 100% vegan and cruelty free. No fish sauce, no shrimp paste, nothing from an animal. Just veggies, gochujang, soy sauce, rice paste and patience.
Why do you call it climate conscious?
Three reasons. It is 100% vegan, and plant-based food generally uses far less water, land and resources than meat. It comes in plastic-free packaging. And every batch is made with fresh ingredients, not cold-stored ones.
Why is the jar fizzy when I open it?
It's alive! Kimchi is naturally fermented with live cultures, and a little fizz or bubbling is what fermentation does. Open it slowly, over a plate, like a responsible earthling.
How do I keep it happy?
Keep it refrigerated (0 to 5°C), always use a clean, dry spoon, and finish it within 4 weeks of opening. Honestly, it won't last that long.
Any allergens I should know about?
It contains soy and wheat (gluten). Soy sauce is usually brewed with wheat and most gochujang contains wheat too, so it isn't suitable for a gluten-free diet. Everything else is in the ingredient list above, nothing hidden.
What do I eat it with?
Straight from the jar, with rice, noodles or as a side. Toss it into stir fries, fried rice or sandwiches. Stuff it into rolls, bowls or salads for a flavour boost.
Is kimchi good for your gut?
Our kimchi is naturally fermented and contains live cultures, and plenty of people enjoy fermented foods as part of a balanced diet. We keep the promises small: real ingredients, slow fermentation, no artificial preservatives.

Right here. Tap Get the Jar on mrearthling.com and the order opens in WhatsApp. We take orders on WhatsApp only and deliver in Bengaluru.
